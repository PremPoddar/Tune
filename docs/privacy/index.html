Privacy policy

Tune and your data

Last updated 22 October 2026

In short: Tune collects nothing and can’t go online — it has no internet permission. Volume levels are kept on your phone.

What Tune collects

Nothing. Tune has no internet permission, so it couldn’t send anything if it wanted to. There are no accounts, analytics or ads.

What stays on your phone

The volume you chose for each app and output, and your settings. Uninstalling Tune deletes them.

What Tune looks at

Which app is in front, and which apps are playing media, so it can set the right level. It never reads what’s on your screen or what your notifications say.

Permissions

What Tune asks Android for, and why:

PermissionWhy
AccessibilityTo know which app is on screen, and to show Tune’s panel when you press the volume buttons. No screen content is read.
Notification accessAndroid only shows other apps’ media sessions to apps with this. No notification is read.
Do Not Disturb accessRequired by Android to change ring and notification volume. Tune never toggles Do Not Disturb.
Modify audio settingsTo set volumes and attach the volume effects that let apps play at different levels.

Children

Tune isn't aimed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here and in the app's source repository, where every change can be seen.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue at github.com/PremPoddar/Tune/issues.
