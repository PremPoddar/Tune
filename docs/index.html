Try it

A mixer for your phone

Slide one app. The rest stay put.

Each app keeps its own level, and Tune keeps a separate set for each place the sound goes. Change outputs on the right and every app jumps to the level you set there.

  • Drag a slider — that app’s level is remembered
  • Switch to headphones or the car and the levels follow
  • “Mix” apps play together at different volumes
  • “Switch” apps get their level whenever they’re playing

Two ways

How Tune controls each app

Android has one media volume, so Tune labels every app with the method it gets.

ModeWhich appsWhat happens
MixPlayers that share their audio: Spotify, YouTube Music, Poweramp, VLC…Tune sets each one’s own level, and they play together at different volumes.
SwitchEverything else: YouTube, browsers, most gamesTune moves the phone’s media volume to the app’s level whenever it’s the one playing.
Exact (optional)Every app, with Shizuku runningTune scales each app’s own player, like the system does when it ducks audio.

Under the hood

How it works

01

Turn Tune on

Allow the accessibility and notification access it asks for. Tune only uses them to know which app is in front and what’s playing.

02

Use your phone

Set each app’s volume the normal way. Tune remembers it, per output.

03

Forget about it

From then on, every app comes back at its own level by itself.
